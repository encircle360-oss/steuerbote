{#- The one stylesheet of the print formats of Steuerbote: A4, black and grey, a font with the metrics of Helvetica that the server has, classes of their own under .sb-print, since Frappe's print stylesheet styles bare classes such as label. The PDFs come from wkhtmltopdf, which takes its page margins from a rule on .print-format alone, ignores @page margins and page-break-after, and keeps rows and blocks with page-break-inside: avoid. The sheet does not rely on Frappe's print bundle, which a site may fail to load into wkhtmltopdf. A print format includes it first, then the running head and foot, and wraps its content in
. The gap between the running head and the content is padding at the bottom of the head, not the page option for the spacing of the header: Frappe 16.60 hands that option to its Chromium-based generator as text and fails, while both generators measure the head with its padding. -#}
