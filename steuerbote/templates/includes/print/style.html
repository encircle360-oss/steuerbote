The PDFs come from wkhtmltopdf, which takes its page margins from a rule on .print-format alone, ignores @page margins and page-break-after, and keeps rows and blocks with page-break-inside: avoid. The sheet does not rely on Frappe's print bundle, which a site may fail to load into wkhtmltopdf. A print format includes it first, then the running head and foot, and wraps its content in
. The head is as high as the top margin and the foot as high as the bottom margin, the gaps to the content inside them, so that both generators put them in the same place: wkhtmltopdf sets them into the margins, the Chromium-based generator stacks them on margins of 0 (chromium.html). Neither sets the page option for the spacing of the header: Frappe 16.60 hands it to its Chromium-based generator as text and fails. Line heights, the padding of cells and the rules of tables are whole pixels: wkhtmltopdf rounds each down to a pixel and Chromium keeps the fraction, so that with fractions every row is higher with Chromium and its printouts run to more pages. A line outside a cell is at least 1.39 times as high as its text: Nimbus Sans rises 0.729 of its size, the dots of a bold Ü 0.922, and Chromium paints what stands above the box of the first line of a page onto the foot of the page before. -#} {%- if steuerbote_pdf_generator() == "chrome" %} {% include "steuerbote/templates/includes/print/chromium.html" %} {%- endif %}
